// C8: view models reach the clipboard and the shell through a service.
T:System.Windows.Clipboard;Use IClipboardService, or ClipboardService.Shared in XAML-created views: another application can hold the clipboard open at any moment (C8).
M:System.Diagnostics.Process.Start(System.String);Pass a ProcessStartInfo to run a tool. To open a URL or reveal a file, use IDesktopService (C8).
M:System.Diagnostics.Process.Start(System.String,System.String);Pass a ProcessStartInfo to run a tool. To open a URL or reveal a file, use IDesktopService (C8).
M:System.Diagnostics.Process.Start(System.String,System.Collections.Generic.IEnumerable{System.String});Pass a ProcessStartInfo to run a tool. To open a URL or reveal a file, use IDesktopService (C8).
P:System.Diagnostics.ProcessStartInfo.UseShellExecute;False is already the default, so delete the assignment. Shell navigation belongs to IDesktopService (C8).
